Custom Paver Driveways

Beautiful, Durable Paver Driveways

Your driveway is the first thing anyone sees, and in northern Michigan it's also the hardest working surface on your property. It takes plow blades, salt, freeze and thaw, and the weight of every vehicle that visits.

Concrete cracks. Asphalt softens and fades. A properly built paver driveway handles all of it, and when something does eventually need attention, individual pavers can be lifted and reset rather than tearing out a whole slab.

We design driveway layouts and patterns that suit the house, whether that’s formal and symmetrical or something warmer with a border and inlay. Homeowners tell us regularly that the driveway changed how the entire property reads from the street.

Frequently Asked Questions

We Can Answer All Your Questions

What does an outdoor living space cost in Traverse City? 

Every relationship and every project is unique, so we don’t publish a price range that would be wrong for most people who read it. Cost depends on square footage, material selection, site conditions, grade, drainage needs, and features like fireplaces, kitchens, or lighting. After we see your space, you get a detailed written quote with no obligation, and that price is locked for 30 days. 

How long does an outdoor living space project take? 

It depends on scope and weather, and we won't pretend otherwise. A straightforward patio and seating area moves faster than a full outdoor kitchen with a fireplace and integrated lighting. What we will do is give you a specific timeline in writing during your free consultation, before you commit to anything, and tell you right away if weather is going to shift it. 

How long will your crew be on my property? 

We give you the on-site window in your written estimate, and we hold to it unless weather forces a change. When that happens, you hear it from us the same day. We keep the work area clean and safe the entire time, because you still have to live there while we’re working.
